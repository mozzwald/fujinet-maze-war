# 04-02 — Give the local actor a render-only position

## Why

`LOCX/LOCY` is simultaneously the rendered position, the collision position and
the shot origin. There is no render-only state, so there is nothing to smooth
into — which is why every correction so far has had to be either a teleport or,
since `LOCAL_FOLLOW`, a walk that moves *gameplay* state one cell at a time.
The walk is a stopgap: it makes the correction look better by making the
simulation lie for several ticks. This plan replaces it with the real
separation.

## Shape

Add render X/Y alongside `LOCX/LOCY`. The simulation position is set
immediately from authority; the render position chases it. Draw and erase read
render state; collision, `NET_AHEAD_FREE`, shot origin and occupancy keep
reading simulation state.

## Watch out for

- The erase path is the trap. Every draw has a matching erase, and this phase
  has already produced three separate bugs from draw and erase disagreeing
  about which cells an actor occupies (`SETSUIT` sub-cell offset, the left/up
  shot spill, the `ERASHOT` mirror). Whatever the render position is, the erase
  must be driven by the same value that drew, not recomputed from simulation.
- `ERASMAN` blanks two characters at the actor's cell. With render and
  simulation positions differing, "the actor's cell" is ambiguous — pick one
  and make it explicit in the code, not implicit in which variable was handy.
- Retire `LOCAL_FOLLOW` in this plan rather than leaving both mechanisms live.

## Done when

- Corrections read as motion rather than a jump, judged by the user.
- The source guard passes: no gameplay decision site references render state.
- `NET_GLIDE_ON` and `LOCAL_FOLLOW` are gone, with their behaviour subsumed.

## Result

Landed in two commits.

`2deb12b` split the state. The finding that shaped it: `LOCX/LOCY` was never a
cell coordinate. `PRVXADD/PRVYADD` are -1 for left/up and `AFTXADD/AFTYADD` are
+1 for right/down, so it committed at the *start* of a left or up move and the
*end* of a right or down one, and any gameplay decision taken mid-animation read
a position a cell off in a direction that depended on facing. `LOCX/LOCY` is now
the simulation cell, stepped whole at `INITMVE` via new `DIRXADD/DIRYADD`;
`RNDX/RNDY` keeps the stagger and drives every draw. The halves still sum to the
whole, which is what makes the two meet at rest.

`363cd4d` retired the walk. `NET_RCHASE_ARM` puts authority into `LOCX/LOCY` at
once and deliberately leaves `RNDX/RNDY` alone; `RENDER_CHASE` walks the picture
in through the ordinary animation, with `NET_RCHASE_STEP` telling `INITMVE` not
to step the simulation a second time. `LOCAL_FOLLOW` and `NET_GLIDE_ON` are
gone.

Verification of the chase needed the server's `--lag-ms` aid, because at zero lag
corrections are too rare to exercise it — the first attempt armed a chase exactly
once. At 450ms: 60 chases in one scripted run, all converged, longest 18 ticks,
largest gap 5 cells (inside `NET_GLIDE_MAX`, so the walk covered every one and
the snap path was never needed), no stuck chase, 290 cells of movement. At zero
lag, 3 corrections over 498 cells.

User confirmed on hardware after the first commit: "movement feels the same, no
sluggishness."

## Method notes

Two measurement errors, both worth remembering:

- **Blocked A/B is not safe on this rig.** Running three runs of one build then
  three of the other let session-to-session drift land entirely on one arm and
  produced a convincing 5x "regression" that did not exist. Strictly alternating
  the builds, one run each, removed it. A question was put to the user on the
  strength of the bad numbers.
- **Symbol addresses move.** Adding `RNDX/RNDY` shifted `MOVEST` from 172 to
  180, and a probe kept reading 180 against the older build, reporting zero
  moves. Same class as the `ACTFLAG` error already in STATE.md: look symbols up
  in `build/maze-war.lab` per build, not once.

Also: the scripted cell count depends on spawn position and is not comparable
across rejoins — the same comparison gave opposite answers on consecutive
attempts. It is usable within one continuous session only.
